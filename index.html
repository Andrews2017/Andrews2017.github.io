Andre Niyongabo Rubungo

I am a 5th-year PhD candidate in Computer Science at Princeton University, advised by Prof. Adji Bousso Dieng. My research interests are large language models and AI for Science, with a focus on leveraging large language models for scientific discovery.

My research internships at IBM Research span LLM post-training (2025) and agentic coding models (2026). Previously, I worked on low-resource machine translation at Polytechnic University of Catalonia, Barcelona Tech (UPC) under the supervision of Prof. Marta R. Costa-jussà and Dr. Carlos Escolano and interned with Meta AI’s NLLB team under the supervision of Angela Fan. I earned my bachelor’s and master’s degrees in Computer Science at the University of Electronic Science and Technology of China (UESTC).

Research

My work connects three themes:

  • Language models for materials discovery. Developing representations and models to predict crystal properties and metal–organic framework free energies (LLM-Prop, MOF free energy prediction).
  • Evaluating scientific prediction. Building benchmarks and testing how well models generalize to materials beyond their training data (LLM4Mat-Bench, out-of-distribution generalization).
  • Language modeling and retrieval for low-resource languages. Using linguistic structure to improve language representations and retrieval-augmented generation (KinyaBERT, KinyaColBERT).
